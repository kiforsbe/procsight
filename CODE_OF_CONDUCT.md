# Code of conduct

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests and other project-managed
community spaces, including interactions with users, contributors and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and welcome different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique code and ideas with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect others' time, boundaries and privacy. Do not publish personal information
  or sensitive process data without permission.
- Accept corrections and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation,
personal attacks and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For visible misconduct, use the platform's reporting tools when available. A private
maintainer conduct-reporting address has not yet been configured. You may request a
private contact route without naming affected people or disclosing sensitive details;
do not post a sensitive conduct report publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this reporting process.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock disruptive
conversations, warn participants, or restrict participation according to severity and
repetition. Reports should be handled with discretion, fairness and attention to the
safety of affected people. Platform operators may apply their own rules independently.

No response-time or confidentiality guarantee is made for public platform reports.
